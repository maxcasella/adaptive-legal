The short version

Baseline holds your training so it can write you a program and so your friends can see what you did. It does not track you, carries no advertising or analytics code, and sells nothing to anybody. The only server it talks to is ours, and the one outside company that sees any of your writing is Anthropic, which is the coach that writes your program.

What we collect

Your Apple account identifier and email address. You sign in with Apple. Apple sends us a stable identifier for you and this app, and an email address, which may be one of Apple's private relay addresses. We use the identifier to know whose training is whose. We use the email only to reach you about your account. It is never shown to other people.

The name you choose. Friends see a name you pick. Nothing requires it to be your real name. It is the only name we hold; the name on your phone is never sent to us.

Your training. Sessions, the loads and times you record, your marks, your body weight, and the programs written for you. This is the app.

What you write. The "How are you feeling?" box, the note after a session, your answers during intake, anything you type to the coach, and comments you leave on other people's training. Some of that describes how your body feels, so we treat it as health information.

Sleep, heart rate variability and resting heart rate, if you connect Apple Health. Read only. We never write anything back to Health, and we ask for nothing beyond those three figures. If you use a Whoop, a Garmin, an Oura ring or an Apple Watch, those reach us the same way: through Health. We have no direct connection to any of them.

A notification token. If you turn notifications on, Apple gives us a token for that device so we know where to send them. It is kept per device, because you might be signed in on a phone and an iPad.

A photo, if you choose one. Your avatar is cut down on your phone and stays there. It travels only inside your backup, which we store as bytes we do not open. Nobody else ever sees it.

Where it goes

Your phone. Everything is on your phone first. The app works with no connection.

Our server. Your backup, so a new phone gets your training back. We store it as an opaque archive and do not read it. Separately, your marks are stored in readable columns, because comparing a 5K with a friend is only possible if we can read both. Your friendships, the cards you share, reactions, comments and reports are stored so they can reach the people they are for.

Anthropic. Writing a program means sending your training profile and what you have written about how it is going to Anthropic's Claude models. That includes the free text above. Anthropic processes it to answer and does not train models on it. This is the one third party that sees your writing, and it is what makes the product work.

Apple. Sign in with Apple, and push notifications.

Other people, only where you asked. Friends see the cards you share, your name, your avatar and your marks. They do not see your email, your body weight, what you typed in the feeling box, or anything you wrote to the coach.

What we do not do

We do not track you across apps or websites. There is no advertising code and no analytics code in the app. We do not sell or rent anything about you. We do not build a profile of you for anybody else.

How long we keep it

Your training stays until you delete it. Your session with us lasts 30 days, after which the app asks Apple who you are again. We keep the 50 most recent shared cards per account and the three most recent backups; older ones are dropped automatically.

Deleting it

Settings, Account, Delete your account. It deletes rather than deactivates: your account, your backups, your marks, your friendships, your cards and anything you wrote on somebody else's card all go, and the deletion cascades in the database rather than being a flag. Export your training first if you want to keep it; there is a row above for that.

Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. The app permits no unencrypted connection to anything. Your session token is kept in the device keychain.

Children

Baseline is not intended for anybody under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app rather than only here.
