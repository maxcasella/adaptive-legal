Using Baseline

Baseline writes you a training program and keeps a record of what you did. You need an Apple account to sign in, and you have to be 13 or older.

Baseline is not medical advice

Baseline is a training app. It is not a doctor, a physiotherapist or any other kind of clinician, and nothing it says is a diagnosis, a treatment or medical advice. The programs it writes come from a model, reviewed against a knowledge base, and no human checks any individual program before you see it.

You decide whether a session is safe for you. If something hurts, stop. If you are not sure whether you should be training at all, ask somebody qualified before you start, and especially before you start something new.

When you tell Baseline about a symptom, one of two things happens. For anything that could be cardiac, neurological or otherwise urgent, Baseline refuses to write or change a program and tells you to see a medical professional. For an injury it can reasonably work around, it writes a program around the area and tells you to get it looked at first. Neither of those is a clinical judgement about you. The first is not a diagnosis, and the second is not a clearance to train. In both cases Baseline cannot take responsibility for an injury getting worse.

Baseline does not know everything about you. It knows what you told it. If something has changed, tell it, and if something matters, tell a clinician rather than an app.

What you put in it

What you write stays yours. You give us permission to store it, to show it to the friends you chose to share it with, and to send it to the coach so a program can be written. That is the whole of it.

Do not post anything about another person that you would not want posted about you. There is no tolerance for harassment or threats. Every card and every comment can be reported and every person can be blocked, from anywhere their name appears. Reports are read by a person. An account that is used to harass somebody can be removed without notice.

Your friends

A friendship is an agreement between two people and either of them can end it. Blocking somebody ends the friendship, takes their name and their words off your training, keeps them from finding you and does not tell them. You can turn off being findable by name entirely in Settings.

Your account

You can export everything and you can delete everything, both from Settings. Deleting deletes; it does not deactivate.

We can suspend or remove an account that is being used to abuse somebody else, or to attack the service.

What we promise, and what we do not

Baseline is provided as it is. We work hard to keep it running and to keep your training safe, and we do not promise it will never be unavailable, never lose anything, or produce a program that suits you.

To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for injury, loss or damage arising from training you chose to do, or from the app being unavailable. Nothing here limits any liability that cannot be limited by law, and your statutory rights are unaffected.
